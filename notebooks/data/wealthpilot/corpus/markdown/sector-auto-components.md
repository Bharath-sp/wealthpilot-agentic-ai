# Sector Underwriting Note: Auto Components

**ASHVA CAPITAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**  
**PART IV: SECTOR-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES**  
**SUB-SECTION 8.14: AUTO COMPONENTS SME LENDING**  

---

### 8.14.1 SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This underwriting note applies to all working capital, term loan, and equipment financing applications originated for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) operating within the Auto Components sub-sector (NAICS 3363 / NIC 29301), including Tier-2 and Tier-3 suppliers engaged in precision machining, metal stamping, plastic injection molding, electrical wiring harnesses, and rubber/silicone seals. 

---

### 8.14.2 SECTOR WORKING CAPITAL CYCLE
Underwriters shall evaluate working capital facilities based on a baseline Net Working Capital (NWC) cycle of **100 calendar days**, structured as follows:

1. **Raw Material Inventory Holding:** 35 days  
2. **Work-in-Progress (WIP) Holding:** 15 days  
3. **Finished Goods Holding:** 20 days  
4. **Receivables Realization (OEM / Tier-1 Buyers):** 75 days  
5. **Trade Payables Window:** 45 days  

*Operational Rule:* Any borrower exhibiting a Total Working Capital Cycle exceeding **120 days** is strictly ineligible for automated credit scoring and must be escalated to the Senior Credit Committee for manual cash-flow stress testing.

---

### 8.14.3 DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (DSCR) STANDARDS
To buffer against OEM margin compression and capital expenditure demands, the sector-specific minimum DSCR for Auto Component SMEs is established at **1.35x**, overriding Ashva Capital’s standard baseline requirement of **1.25x**.

* **1.35x DSCR Floor:** Mandated for all facilities up to $3.0M.
* **1.45x DSCR Floor:** Mandated for borrowers where >40% of revenues are derived from the Commercial Vehicle (CV) segment, due to higher cyclicality.

---

### 8.14.4 NUMERIC MANDATORY THRESHOLDS
Underwriters are bound by the following non-negotiable operational and financial parameters. Breaches of any single parameter require a formal Risk Waiver signed by the Chief Credit Officer (CCO).

| Parameter | Threshold Limit | Action Upon Breach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DSCR Floor** | **1.35x** | Immediate rejection if <1.25x; CCO waiver required for 1.25x–1.34x |
| **Single-OEM Customer Concentration** | **45.0%** of total gross revenue | Haircut eligible receivables borrowing base by 30% |
| **Max Total Debt / EBITDA** | **3.20x** | Decline facility extension |
| **Minimum Gross Margin** | **14.5%** | Decline facility extension |
| **Maximum Receivables Past-Due Age** | **90 days** from invoice date | Exclude invoices >90 days from collateral borrowing base |
| **Minimum Tangible Net Worth (TNW)** | **$750,000** | Exclude from SME simplified program; transfer to Corporate Risk |

---

### 8.14.5 SEASONALITY ADJUSTMENTS AND STRESS TESTING
The auto components sector experiences seasonal production lulls during annual OEM plant maintenance shutdowns (April and December) and demand surges preceding Q3 model-year rollouts and festive sales periods (August through October).

1. **Cash Flow Testing Model:** Debt service capacity must be tested using a simulated **30.0% drop in monthly revenue** across the months of April and December. The borrower must maintain a minimum monthly Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) of **1.10x** during these two stress months.
2. **Moratorium Structure:** Working Capital demand loans may include a principal repayment holiday during the month of April, provided the borrower deposits **150%** of the debt service obligation for April into a Debt Service Reserve Account (DSRA) no later than March 15.

---

### 8.14.6 PRIMARY CAUSES OF SECTOR DISTRESS
Underwriters must specifically evaluate and document risk mitigants for the two primary drivers of default in this sector:

1. **OEM Price-Down Clauses (Margin Squeeze):** Original Equipment Manufacturers routinely enforce mandatory annual price reductions of **2.0% to 4.0%** on Tier-2/Tier-3 components. The underwriter must verify that the borrower has achieved a minimum **3.0% annual productivity yield gain** or raw material cost reduction over the preceding 24 months to maintain operating margins.
2. **Raw Material Pass-Through Lag:** Price inflation in input commodities (steel, aluminum, synthetic rubber) is passed on to OEMs with a contractual lag of **60 to 90 days**. Underwriters must confirm the presence of an active Price Variation Clause (PVC) in customer contracts. If no PVC exists, the underwriter must apply an additional **250 basis point reduction** to projected operating margins.

---

### 8.14.7 ACCEPTABLE COLLATERAL TYPES AND ADVANCE RATES
Facilities must be secured by first-ranking charges over approved collateral types. Advance rates and haircuts are defined below:

1. **Book Debts (Receivables):** 
   * Approved Tier-1 OEMs / Auto Majors: **70% Advance Rate** (Invoices capped at 75 days).
   * Tier-2 Suppliers / Aftermarket Distributors: **50% Advance Rate** (Invoices capped at 60 days).
2. **Inventory:**
   * Raw Materials (Steel coils, aluminum ingots, standard resins): **50% Advance Rate**.
   * Work-in-Progress (WIP): **0% Advance Rate** (Ineligible collateral).
   * Finished Goods: **40% Advance Rate** (Subject to a 60-day aging limit).
3. **Plant & Machinery (P&M):**
   * Specialized tooling and dies: **0% Advance Rate** (Ineligible due to zero re-sale market).
   * General purpose CNC machines, heavy stamping presses (>200 ton), and injection molding machinery: Maximum **50% LTV** based on Orderly Liquidation Value (OLV) established by an Ashva-approved appraiser within the last 90 days.
4. **Industrial Real Estate:** Maximum **65% LTV** against appraised Fair Market Value (FMV).

---

### 8.14.8 SECTOR EXPOSURE CAP AND CONCENTRATION LIMITS
To prevent systemic portfolio risk, Ashva Capital enforces strict portfolio-level exposure caps on the Auto Components SME sector:

1. **Maximum Sector Portfolio Hold:** The aggregate outstanding exposure (funded and non-funded) to the Auto Components SME sector shall not exceed **8.5%** of Ashva Capital’s total live SME loan book.
2. **Sub-Segment Concentration Cap:** Exposure to borrowers exclusively serving the Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) powertrain segment is capped at **3.0%** of total SME portfolio book value, reflecting energy transition risk.
3. **Cap Breach Governance:** If sector exposure reaches **8.0%** (the Warning Threshold), the Portfolio Risk Officer shall lock automated originations for the sector. Approvals beyond 8.0% up to the 8.5% hard cap require joint sign-off from the Chief Credit Officer (CCO) and the Chief Risk Officer (CRO).
